## Coverage

- **188 of 188 cases reviewed.** All PRE and BREAKING logs were present. 172 cases have PRE/BREAKING evidence archives. The other 16 (Cases 1–16) were reviewed from the logs, plus the JAR and source evidence that exists for Cases 15 and 16.
- **No full reruns were performed.** The existing logs, surefire reports, JAR inspections and probes were enough to isolate the initiating failure in every case.
- **Targeted checks (added 2026-11-20).** 11 checks ran afterwards in fresh containers from the BREAKING images: 8 dependency trees, 1 capture of the surefire dump, and 2 single-change controls. All 11 agree with the classification evidence (see `reruns/RESULTS.md`).
  - **Case 69:** aligning the retained GraalJS `js` artifact to 22.1.0 makes all 80 tests pass. This supports NON_BEHAVIORAL for a disputed case.
  - **Case 30:** adding the missing ASM provider makes all 737 tests pass.
  - **Case 116:** the hidden error is `Unsupported version of junit:junit: 3.8.1`.
- **Method.** The blind pass read only primary evidence: logs, archives, POMs, JARs and probe outputs, with label-like keys removed from evidence JSON. The labels were frozen and hashed before any Codex label or rationale was read.
- **Exposure.** Codex labels for Cases 161 and 162 were seen during the workspace survey. Both are flagged in `exposure_log.md` and in their audit rows.

## Data-quality findings

- **Masked exit codes.** In `batch_triage_results.csv`, `breaking_exit_code` is 0 for 173 builds that failed, because `mvn … | tee` hides Maven's exit status. The archive summaries for the fresh runs (`exit=0`) have the same problem. Outcomes must come from the BUILD lines and reactor summaries.
- **UTF-16 logs.** The logs for Cases 6 and 7 are UTF-16 (written through a PowerShell redirect), so UTF-8 parsers miss their BUILD lines.
- **Duplicate and hidden counts:**
  - Raw XML double-counts in Cases 75, 84, 99, 105, 138, 139, 149, 163 and 168 (duplicate TestNG/JUnit exports). The Maven aggregates are correct.
  - Class-level setup errors hide most of the drop in reported tests in Cases 2, 18 and 132 (291 → 94) and the pay-adminusers cases (757 → 726).
- **Archive coverage gap.** In Case 33, `artifacts.tar` omits the edited module POM; it is archived separately.
- **Verified repair (Case 14).** The repair log shows a full-reactor BUILD SUCCESS (190 tests, 36 skipped), but it never prints the resolved H2 JAR. Recording `dependency:tree` would prove the updated dependency was retained.
- **Later phases.** No retrieval, knowledge-base or repair-agent code exists in the workspace. RQ2–RQ4 remain planned work.

## Limitations

- **One AI reviewer, not independent of the evidence collection.** The probes, JAR inspections and reconciled-count files that Claude relied on were produced by Codex's pipeline, and Claude reviewed their outputs, not their construction.
- **Mostly no runs of its own.** Apart from offline log, archive and JAR inspection, Claude ran nothing. Several "resolved version" statements rest on archived JARs or on error messages rather than on `dependency:tree`; these are noted per case under `resolve_uncertainty`.
- **Partial blindness.** Blindness was incomplete for Cases 161 and 162. Aggregate counts (64/124) were known from the brief.
- **Boundary cases.** The blind pass gave boundary cases a leaning label plus an adjudication note rather than UNCERTAIN. The six ANTLR cases were moved to UNCERTAIN only after the comparison exposed a defensible opposite reading.
- **Mechanism families** were assigned by rule from the primary-cause text and dependency. They are a starting point for split design, not a validated clustering.
